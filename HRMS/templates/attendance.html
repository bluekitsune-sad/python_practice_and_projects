Mark attendance

Late time is set to {{ late_time }} (change it in Settings). Later arrival = status Late. Saving over an existing date updates it.

Pick Approved leave or Absent and arrival/hours are cleared automatically.

How it works

  • Date defaults to today - change it to back-fill a past day. You will see a warning if that day already has data.
  • Status is automatic for worked days: Present (arrival up to {{ late_time }}), Late (after it). Or explicitly mark Approved leave / Absent.
  • Everyone is listed (ex employees shown with an (ex) tag) so you can back-fill their last days; ex employees keep their records and still appear in exports.
  • Any working day you never mark is filled as Absent automatically when you open the Export or Calendar page (off days like Saturday are skipped - choose them in Settings).
